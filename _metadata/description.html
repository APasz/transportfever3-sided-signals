Sided Signals

Configurable placement and function controls for Transport Fever 3 railway signals.

Features

  • Place signal models on their original side or move them to the left or right of the track.
  • Adjust lateral position from -5 to 20 metres in 0.5-metre steps.
  • Adjust position along the track from -20 to 50 metres.
  • Optionally adjust height, yaw, pitch, and roll.
  • Keep the original function or use a signal as a signal or waypoint.
  • Keep, disable, or enable the whistle or horn event for passing trains.
  • Includes the game's standard one-way option for signals.
  • Preserves compatible controls already supplied by other mods.
  • Localised for every language shipped with Transport Fever 3.

Usage

Geometric adjustments appear in the placement toolbar. Side, function, whistle, and one-way controls remain in the construction menu. Default values preserve each signal's original appearance and behaviour.

Advanced height and orientation controls are hidden by default. Use the toolbar toggle to show them for the current session, or change the mod setting to show them whenever a game loads. A separate mod setting selects the default track offset for newly placed signals.

Compatibility

Sided Signals recognises vanilla signals and supported signal mods. The optional compatibility override adds its controls to every track-snapped edge object, including unrecognised third-party constructions. Ordinary assets are not supported because they do not provide the required signal and edge-model data.
